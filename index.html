Frequently Asked Questions

What is HECS-HELP?
HECS-HELP is Australia's student loan scheme. Repayments are compulsory once your income exceeds the minimum threshold ($54,435 in 2024-25).
Is this up to date?
Yes — uses 2024-25 repayment thresholds from the Australian Taxation Office.
Is this free?
Yes — completely free, no sign-up needed.
Does it include indexation?
The payoff estimate includes approximate indexation of ~4% per year on the outstanding debt.
